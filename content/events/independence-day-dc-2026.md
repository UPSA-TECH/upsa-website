Every August 14th, UPSA chapters across the country celebrate Pakistan's Independence Day together.

This year's D.C. celebration will be held at a venue in the National Mall area, with:

- Pakistani food from local restaurants
- Live music and poetry
- Flag hoisting ceremony
- Henna and cultural stalls

Free admission. All are welcome — bring your green and white!
