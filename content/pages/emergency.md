urgentTitle: Start here if there is immediate risk.
urgentDescription: If someone may be hurt, you feel physically unsafe, or there is a medical emergency, call 911. If you are thinking about suicide, self-harm, or cannot calm down from severe distress, call or text 988.
hotlines:
  - name: Immediate Danger
    number: "911"
    note: Call police, fire, or ambulance if you feel unsafe, someone is hurt, or there is immediate danger.
    href: tel:911
  - name: 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
    number: Call or text 988
    note: For suicidal thoughts, severe anxiety, panic, emotional crisis, self-harm concerns, or when you need a trained crisis counselor now.
    href: tel:988
  - name: Crisis Text Line
    number: Text HOME to 741741
    note: Free 24/7 text support if calling feels hard. A trained crisis counselor responds by text.
    href: sms:741741
  - name: SAMHSA National Helpline
    number: 1-800-662-4357
    note: Treatment referral and information for mental health, drug, and alcohol concerns.
    href: tel:18006624357
  - name: National Domestic Violence Hotline
    number: 1-800-799-7233
    note: Confidential safety planning and support for relationship abuse, domestic violence, stalking, or unsafe living situations.
    href: tel:18007997233
  - name: RAINN Sexual Assault Hotline
    number: 1-800-656-4673
    note: Confidential 24/7 support after sexual assault or abuse.
    href: tel:18006564673
sections:
  - cat: Mental Health
    items:
      - title: Campus Counseling Center
        desc: Most U.S. universities have a counseling or psychological services office. Search your university name plus counseling center or CAPS.
      - title: What to Say When You Call
        desc: Say, "I am a student and I need to speak with a counselor. I am dealing with anxiety/stress and need an appointment." If urgent, ask for same-day crisis support.
      - title: If You Are Worried About a Friend
        desc: Stay with them if they may hurt themselves. Call 988 together, contact your RA/resident director, or call campus police for a wellness check.
  - cat: Immigration & Legal Issues
    items:
      - title: Start With Your DSO
        desc: For I-20, SEVIS, CPT, OPT, reduced course load, leave of absence, travel signature, or work authorization issues, contact your Designated School Official immediately.
      - title: Do Not Guess on Immigration Forms
        desc: If there is a deadline, denial, request for evidence, arrest, termination risk, or status problem, speak with a qualified immigration attorney or DOJ-accredited representative.
      - title: Free or Low-Cost Legal Help
        desc: USCIS explains who can legally represent you. LawHelp can help find local free legal aid. Many universities also offer student legal services.
        links:
          - label: USCIS Legal Services
            href: https://www.uscis.gov/scams-fraud-and-misconduct/avoid-scams/find-legal-services
          - label: LawHelp.org
            href: https://www.lawhelp.org/
  - cat: Housing, Food, and Money
    items:
      - title: Emergency Housing
        desc: Contact your Dean of Students office, international student office, campus housing, and local chapter leaders. Ask about emergency housing, temporary dorm placement, and hardship funds.
      - title: Food Assistance
        desc: Many campuses have free food pantries and meal-swipe emergency programs. For local help, call 211 or use HUD Find Shelter.
        links:
          - label: HUD Find Shelter
            href: https://www.hud.gov/findshelter
      - title: Unsafe Roommate or Lease Situation
        desc: If you feel unsafe, leave first and call 911 or campus police. Then contact housing, student legal services, and the Dean of Students.
  - cat: Physical Safety and Reporting
    items:
      - title: If You Feel Unsafe
        desc: Call 911 for immediate danger. On campus, also save campus police/security and your RA/residence hall front desk.
      - title: Domestic or Dating Violence
        desc: Abuse can be physical, emotional, sexual, digital, immigration-related, or financial. The National Domestic Violence Hotline can help with safety planning.
      - title: Sexual Assault
        desc: RAINN provides confidential support and can explain medical, reporting, and local options. Campus Title IX offices can also explain accommodations.
        links:
          - label: RAINN Hotline
            href: https://rainn.org/help-and-healing/hotline
reachTitle: Still need help? Reach out to UPSA.
reachTitleEmphasis: Reach out to UPSA
reachDescription: Our team and chapter network can help you think through next steps, find campus offices, locate community support, or connect with alumni. For emergencies, use 911 or the relevant hotline first.
