## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:
- Responding to inquiries and providing requested services
- Sending newsletters and event updates (with your consent)
- Improving website functionality and user experience

- Emergency contact purposes (as provided in forms)

## 5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information, including:
- Secure HTTPS connection for the website
- Limited access to personal data
- Regular security assessments
- Compliance with standard nonprofit data protection practices

However, no online transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to:

You can control cookie settings in your browser. Disabling cookies may affect website functionality.
