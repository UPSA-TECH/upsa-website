## 13. Mentor Portal

If you create an account on the UPSA Mentor Portal, we collect additional information specific to that feature.

**Who can join.** Anyone can create a portal account. We don't check whether someone is a UPSA member or verify where they work or study, so treat what other members say about themselves the way you would on any networking site.

**Account & sign-in.** Sign-in is handled by Clerk, our authentication provider. Your email address and password are managed by Clerk; we never store your password. We don't save your email address in our database either: we look it up from Clerk only at the moment we send you a notification email (see below).

**Profile information.** Your name, photo, headline, university and major, company and role, industry, location, bio, skills, education, work experience, certifications, whether you're listed as a mentor/professional, and whether you're open to referral requests. Once you finish setting up your profile, it's visible to every signed-in portal member, including in search. You can hide your profile at any time from Settings ("Deactivate account"), and turn it back on whenever you like.

**Profile photo.** Stored with our file storage provider (Cloudflare R2). Anyone who can see your profile can see your photo.

**Listing yourself as a mentor/professional.** This is self-serve: you add your company and job title and turn the listing on yourself. No one at UPSA reviews or approves it, and we can't verify anyone's employer. Instead, any member can report a profile they think is fake. Once enough different members report the same profile, it's automatically flagged, and accounts can be suspended.

**Resume.** Never shown on your profile. A resume is only shared when you send it to a specific connection in a chat, and only you and that person can download it. It's stored separately from photos, in a location with no public access. If the person you sent it to later deletes their account, the file may stay in our storage even though no one can download it anymore.

**Connection requests and messages.** When you send a request or a message, we store it so the conversation works. It's visible to you, the other member, and UPSA administrators who run the portal (the same way an email administrator could technically access a work inbox), and never to other members or the public. We also record when you last opened each conversation, so it can show you what's unread. The other member can't see this.

**Blocking.** You can block any member from their profile or from a chat. Once you block someone, neither of you can see the other's profile, send the other a request, or message the other, and any connection between you is ended. The other person isn't told. You can unblock them at any time from Settings.

**Reports.** When you report a profile, administrators can see who made the report and the reason you gave. The member you reported can't.

**Notification emails.** We email you when you receive a connection request, have one accepted, or get a new message. These emails only say that something happened and link back to the portal. They never include the content of a request or message. You can turn these off at any time in Settings.

**Deleting your portal account.** This permanently removes your profile, photo, education, experience, certifications, connection requests, connections, and every conversation you're part of, including your messages and the other person's messages in that conversation. Deleting your account removes the whole conversation for both people, not just your side of it. Resumes you sent are deleted too. Copies may remain in our backups until those backups expire.
